Welcome!

AxisAura Lab — a laboratory exploring new physical principles of data transmission. Not bits — but keys. Not calculations — but addressing. Not heating silicon — but using its cold geometry.

We believe: science is a conversation, not a monologue. An idea matters more than a diploma. A prototype matters more than a review. Respect matters more than status.

On these pages — the story of a new method. From the first thought to a working prototype. With blueprints, formulas, debates with the "greats" and, of course, with Shurik.
